Hermes Raspberry Pi

This is a personal assistant operated by Suresh Murali on a Raspberry Pi. It connects to the owner's Google account only with the owner's permission.

The assistant can read and organize Gmail messages, send email at the owner's request, and read or manage Google Calendar events. The dashboards are available only to devices authorized on the owner's private Tailscale network. This public page does not provide access to the assistant or Google data.

For details about data handling, read the Privacy Policy.
